# Chapter 1: The Storm

The wind howled against the shutters. Rain hammered the *old* glass.

> **Alice:** Do you hear that?
> **Tom:** It's only the sea.

The old man looked up from the fire.

> **Old Man:** No. That's the bell.
> Nobody has rung it in forty years.
